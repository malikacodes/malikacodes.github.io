Ring Deck privacy

Ring Deck is a Stream Deck plugin for my own Mac. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it reads. My daily sleep, readiness, activity, stress, SpO2, heart health and resilience summaries for the last 14 days, my latest heart rate and ring battery level, and my basic profile to check the sign-in worked. It only reads. It can't change anything in my Oura account.

Where it goes. Nowhere but my Mac. The plugin talks to Oura and nothing else. The numbers are only kept in memory while the plugin runs, and the sign-in is saved on my Mac, under my user account. There are no analytics and no server of mine, and nothing is shared or sold.

Turning it off. Remove the plugin, then remove Ring Deck from the apps connected to my Oura account.

Questions. Open an issue on github.com/malikacodes.
