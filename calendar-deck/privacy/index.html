Calendar Deck privacy

Calendar Deck is a Stream Deck plugin for my own Mac. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it reads. My list of Google calendars and which ones are checked, plus the events on them for the next 7 days, or 30 days for my birthday calendar. It asks Google for read-only access, so it can't add, change or delete anything.

Where it goes. Nowhere but my Mac. The plugin talks to Google and nothing else. The sign-in and any calendar data it keeps stay on my Mac, under my user account. There are no analytics and no server of mine, and nothing is shared or sold.

Turning it off. Remove the plugin, then remove Calendar Deck at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions. Open an issue on github.com/malikacodes.
